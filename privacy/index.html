Privacy policy

Effective 31 July 2026

Whittle works without an account. It does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party software development kits.

Information kept on your device

Whittle can store the information you choose to provide: your BT postcode district, an approximate coordinate, usual oil order size, recorded oil fills, electricity usage and tariff details, switch confirmations, alert rules, fired alerts, and display preferences. This information is held in the app's private storage on your device. Whittle does not upload it to the developer or include it in price-feed requests.

Location

Whittle asks for location access only after you tap Use my location. It uses the location to derive a BT postcode district and estimate distance to oil suppliers. It keeps the district and a coordinate rounded to roughly 100 metres on your device. It does not monitor location in the background.

Apple's geocoding service may process the temporary location fix to return a postcode under Apple's privacy terms. Whittle does not receive or keep a full address.

Network access

Whittle downloads public heating-oil and electricity price files over HTTPS. Those requests do not contain your district, location, household information, or advertising identifier. GitHub Pages processes ordinary network information, such as an IP address, as needed to deliver these files; Whittle does not receive or use that information for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

If you choose a supplier's Call or Site action, iOS opens the Phone app or your browser. Information you then provide is governed by that supplier's and the browser's privacy terms.

Notifications

Price rules are evaluated by Whittle and delivered as local notifications. Whittle does not use a push-notification server and does not send alert history off your device.

Retention and deletion

Information remains on your device until you change it or delete the app. Start again clears household setup and preferences but deliberately keeps recorded fills. Deleting Whittle removes all of its on-device data. There is no server-side Whittle account or profile to delete.

Children

Whittle is a household price-information app and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date. For privacy or support questions, use the Whittle support page. Do not include private household information in a public support request.
