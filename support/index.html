Whittle support

Help with Northern Ireland heating-oil quotes and electricity tariffs.

Prices can change between collections. Always confirm a price and its terms with the supplier before ordering or switching.

Before contacting support

  • Check that your BT postcode district is entered in Settings → Heating oil.
  • Pull down on Home to ask Whittle to refresh both price categories.
  • If notifications are off, open Settings → Notifications in iOS and allow notifications for Whittle.
  • Whittle deliberately has no account. Reinstalling the app starts with a new on-device record.

Contact

Open the monitored Whittle support form. GitHub requires sign-in to prevent anonymous spam and will send the maintainer a notification.

Include the Whittle version, iOS version, the screen you were using, and the wording of any error. Do not send a full address, meter number, bill, payment information, or other sensitive household data. Support requests are public; remove anything you would not want published.
